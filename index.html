About

Effiong Itoro John popularly known as Praise John or Slim girl, is an origin of Akwa Ibom state in Nigeria. She is a model and influencer born on 20th July, 2002 thereby making her 23 years of age at this moment.

She is known for her confident dressing style, beauty and bold takes on social matters through her social media pages, her vast engagements and massive follower base on social media says a lot about her influence.

She has featured brands even on large public billboards to help boost their marketing flow.

Praise John is a graduate of the University of Port Harcourt and currently resides in Lagos, Nigeria.
